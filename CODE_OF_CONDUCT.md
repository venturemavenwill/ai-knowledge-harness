# Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

AI Knowledge Harness welcomes people across backgrounds, experience levels,
disciplines, countries, and tool preferences. We commit to a community where
people can exchange evidence, question assumptions, and improve the project
without harassment or intimidation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique claims and changes rather than the people proposing them.
- Distinguish observed evidence from inference, preference, and model output.
- Welcome good-faith questions and help newcomers learn the contribution model.
- Respect privacy, confidentiality, licensing, and attribution.
- Disclose meaningful conflicts of interest.
- Accept maintainer decisions gracefully while using the documented appeal path.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, or personal attacks.
- Doxxing or sharing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberately submitting secrets, confidential code, customer data, or
  improperly licensed material.
- Impersonating a person, organization, or autonomous system.
- Manipulating reviews, evidence, stars, search results, or community metrics.
- Repeated disruption after a maintainer asks for a behavior to stop.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews,
and other spaces where someone represents the project. It applies equally to
human-authored and AI-assisted participation. The human account submitting
content remains responsible for it.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report urgent platform abuse through
[GitHub's abuse reporting tools](https://support.github.com/contact/report-abuse).
For project-specific conduct concerns, contact the maintainer using a private
contact method listed on the
[@venturemavenwill GitHub profile](https://github.com/venturemavenwill).
Do not expose sensitive conduct reports in a public issue.

Maintainers will review reports in context and may edit or remove content, issue
a warning, limit participation, lock a thread, or ban an account. Enforcement
decisions will prioritize community safety, proportionality, and documented
project policy. A person affected by a decision may request one private
reconsideration with relevant context.
